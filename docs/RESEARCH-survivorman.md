## Scenario landscape (short form)
> **Correction (2nd research pass, verified vs Wikipedia/TVDB episode lists):** an earlier digest listed Borneo/Fiji/Yucatan — those Survivorman episodes **do not exist** (confused with *Survivor*). Corrected list below. Episode numbers fixed too.

- **Arctic — Pond Inlet, Baffin Island (S1E5)**: our chosen scenario. Sea ice/snow 700 km N of the Arctic Circle; snowmobile-breakdown frame. Snow-melt water tradeoff (eating snow while resting drops body heat); food brutal (seal hook at breathing holes, willow buds, bone snare; lean-meat "protein starvation" without fat); friction fire impractical → **3-match economy**; igloo built with wrong-season snow = skill check with degraded outcome; polar-bear tension; rescue-NPC ending.
- **Costa Rica — Osa Peninsula (S1E3)**: 2nd-pass researcher's overall pick (dual-zone beach+jungle map, purification-not-scarcity water, tide-gated foraging, raised-platform shelter, reliable bow-drill). Heat/infection-heavy; user chose Arctic instead.
- **Arizona Sonoran Desert (S1E2)**: worst fit — whole episode is water procurement (exactly the thirst-chore we veto).
- **Canadian Rockies (S1E6)**: wet-alpine friction-fire struggle + genuine SAR search; best rescue-signal system to borrow (we did: search passes + detection).
- **Sierra Nevada (S3E1)**: two-sided SAR mechanic (signals attract vs terrain hides) — borrowed as our detection model.
- **Tiburón Island (S4E3/4)**: pure water-deprivation (solar stills) — vetoed for the same reason as the desert.
- **Amazon (S2E2)** / **PNG (S3E6)** / **Aitutaki (S2E6)** / **Himalayan foothills (S7E5)**: single-match fire economy, gear-wetness, shipwreck-loot, apex-stalker AI respectively — each one interesting mechanic, none a better MVP frame than Arctic.

## Researcher's warning about Arctic (important — design around it)
> "The Arctic episode's core loop is fragile: if fire fails, you die; if it succeeds, you coast. The episode's drama is almost entirely hypothermia."
Mitigations we adopt:
1. Cold = **resource-management pressure, not a death timer** (forgiving bands + debuffs; matches needs-research rules).
2. **Secondary pressures always live**: food scarcity (snares/fishing skill), rescue signal (day-7 arc), storms (wetness events), injury/infection, wolves.
3. Fire is multi-stage and *repairable* — losing the fire is a crisis, not a run-over (embers can be nursed; failure costs a night, not the run).

## Real-episode facts worth stealing (Arctic, 2nd-pass detail)
- **Match economy**: exactly 3 matches, each an irreversible spend (qulliq vs signal fire) — candidate post-MVP difficulty mode.
- **Fat vs lean meat** ("rabid starvation"): calorie *quality* as a nutrition axis — post-MVP.
- Seal hunting at breathing holes = patience/stealth stamina mechanic; polar bear = scripted event where defense gear can't be used for food.

(Original 1st-pass notes:)
- Freshwater lake at camp → drinking water trivially available; the *game* decision is fuel cost of melting/boiling vs. the core-temp cost of eating snow / drinking cold.
- Spruce-bough bed as insulation platform = the single most important shelter build (ground steal kills).
- Emergency blanket + debris = hybrid shelter; fire reflector wall (rocks) doubles fire value.
- Snowshoes from boughs/wood for deep snow (candidate later feature).
- Signal fire on shore + being seen moving = actual rescue mechanism (helicopter).
- Ptarmigan (snared with wire), snowshoe hare (snares), fish (improvised) — matches our food trio.
- Rain, not snow, was the misery: wet clothing + wind at night. → Our wetness system is the cold vector.
